**Rós Madair turns a rich, interconnected graph into static files you can query.**
Multi-hop relationships, concept hierarchies, numeric and spatial filters — the
questions that normally need a triplestore or a database-and-API answered instead
over plain files on a CDN, or carried offline on a laptop, with **no server
running** and the data **cryptographically signed** so a reader can trust it.

## The opportunity

A serious graph — hundreds of thousands of richly-typed, interlinked records — is
usually trapped behind infrastructure: a backend to stand up, to secure, to pay
for, to keep online. That rules the data out of exactly the places it is most
needed — a field tablet with no signal, a sovereign deployment that can't send
data to a cloud, a public dataset that should outlive any one server.

Rós Madair removes the backend from the read path. A reader compiles a typed query,
fetches only the fraction of the files that question needs (HTTP range reads over a
zone-map), and composes the answer **in the browser** — or offline, from a copy you
hold. Because every snapshot is signed, "no backend" does not mean "no trust": a
reader re-derives the snapshot's id from the bytes on disk and refuses a tampered
one before it answers anything.

That makes a new class of thing cheap: **query a half-million-asset city graph,
instantly, from a USB stick.**

## See it — Aonach Mór

[**Aonach Mór**](/docs/aonach-mor) is the running illustration: a believable
coastal-city resilience corpus — buildings, people, organisations, hazard
footprints, exposure and insurance, woven into one navigable graph. On it, a
responder's question like —

> *which hazard footprints threaten a building run by an organisation that employs a
> keyholder* —

is a genuine **five-hop graph traversal**, resolved over Rós Madair's edge table
(the multi-hop `OnLink` semijoin you can watch step through in
[How it works](/docs/how-it-works/overview)). The **live** demos run the resilience
queries — critical facilities in a flood extent, concept-hierarchy vulnerability,
dependency cascades, a damage overlay composed at query time — on the city corpus;
and the [**763,000-building** build](/docs/aonach-763k) shows the same engine at
real-city scale, each query touching only a sliver of the ~1.3 GB, with nothing
behind it.

## Lineage

Rós Madair is the static companion to
[Alizarin](https://github.com/flaxandteal/alizarin), an ORM for
[Arches](https://www.archesproject.org/) graph data (alizarin crimson and rose
madder are kin pigments from the madder root — live mixing vs. the pre-ground
colour). Arches heritage graphs are its proving ground and the shape it reads, but
the substrate is general: any sufficiently rich, interlinked semantic graph can be
emitted and queried this way. Heritage is the testcase, not the limit.
