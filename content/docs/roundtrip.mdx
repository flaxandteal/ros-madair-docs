The previous pages hand-wrote SQL. This one closes the loop: **alizarin builds the
query, and alizarin receives the result.** Rós Madair is only the middle.

1. **alizarin populates the picker.** On load, the page asks alizarin's RDM cache for
   the *ServiceRole* controlled list and renders every concept **hierarchically** —
   parents and their narrower terms, indented.
2. **Picking a concept builds a query.** Selecting one expands it to *itself and all
   its descendants* (again from alizarin's hierarchy) and emits a
   `DescendantOrSelfOf` predicate — the exact RM `Concept` query, a node-scoped
   semijoin over `concept_index` (the melted concept store) range-joined to the DFS
   `concept_catalog`, not a predicate on any promoted `concept_id` tile column.
3. **Rós Madair executes it** against the tile-row Parquet (duckdb-wasm).
4. **alizarin hydrates the matches** back into typed `Organisation` resources — the
   `sector` you see is a concept resolved by the same RDM cache that populated the
   picker.

Pick **Hospital** and its three narrower roles (*Hospital liaison*, *Triage lead*)
come along — the query widens from one concept id to three, and three Organisations
come back. Pick a leaf and you get just that one. Nothing here is hand-tuned SQL: the
hierarchy is alizarin's, the pruned fetch is Parquet's, the typed result is alizarin's
again.

<Callout title="One id space, both directions">
  The picker offers concepts by label but the query filters by **id** — each concept's
  value id, pulled from the same RDM entry. That's the id ↔ label duality from the
  [reference](/docs/tile-store) discussion: you *select* by id, alizarin *renders* by
  label, and the two never diverge because they come from one cache.
</Callout>
